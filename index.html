How it reads you

Two signals, watched every frame

No video is ever recorded or uploaded. Everything below happens locally, frame by frame, in memory.

🦴

Spine angle

MediaPipe Pose locates the shoulder and ear. The angle between that line and vertical is the slouch signal — the more you lean in, the wider it opens.

👁

Blink rate

MediaPipe Face Mesh + Eye Aspect Ratio catches each blink. A healthy rate sits near 15–20/min — screen-staring quietly drags it down to 5–7.

The signature idea

A growth chart, not a rulebook

Like the height marks on a classroom wall, but instead of centimeters, each mark is a different tolerance, a different voice.

5–9 yrs

"Buddy the Bear says sit up tall! 🦸"

Short attention span, shortest tolerance — the earliest nudge, the most playful voice.

slouch limit: 20s blink floor: 12/min
10–14 yrs

"Small habits add up — straighten up."

Framed as habit-building rather than a scolding — this age responds to streaks, not stories.

slouch limit: 40s blink floor: 10/min
15–18 yrs

"Blink rate has dropped below healthy range."

Longest tolerance, plain data — treated like a self-improvement dashboard, not a nag.

slouch limit: 60s blink floor: 8/min
